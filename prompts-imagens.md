# Imagens a gerar · Nazzan

Higgsfield + Nano Banana 2 (`nano_banana_2`). A geração não rodou nesta versão porque a conta estava sem créditos.

**Regra:** imagem gerada entra só como clima e material (Ambientes e Acabamentos). Nunca como "projeto entregue" nem como bastidor; essas imagens precisam ser fotos reais. Toda imagem passa por revisão humana antes de publicar (DNA, seção 5.6).

Parâmetros comuns: resolução `2k`, sem pessoas, sem texto, madeira como protagonista, luz quente, grão ausente ou muito sutil (DNA 3.3).

### ambiente-closet · 3:4

```
CAMERA: Medium-format camera at ISO 200, tripod at chest height, square to a walk-in closet wall, slight three-quarter angle.
LENS: 45mm at f/5.6, everything from the front doors to the back wall in focus.
LIGHT: Soft morning daylight from a tall window camera-left at 5000K, plus warm 3000K LED strips hidden inside the open shelves. Shadows soft and directional, no flash.
SUBJECT: Floor-to-ceiling custom closet in light natural oak veneer, slim vertical handles in brushed brass, one section of open shelves with folded linen and three pairs of shoes, a central island with a pale stone top.
FOREGROUND: Edge of the island top entering frame-bottom, out of focus.
MIDGROUND: Oak doors with continuous vertical grain, shadow gaps of 3mm between panels.
BACKGROUND: Warm cream wall, window light falling on the floor in a soft rectangle.
MATERIAL BEHAVIOR: Oak with visible open grain and matte finish, brass catching a single soft highlight.
POST BEHAVIOR: Natural color, warm neutral balance, gentle contrast, clean surfaces, no heavy grain.
COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Strong verticals, closet filling two thirds of frame, quiet negative space top-right.
MOOD & ART DIRECTION: Composition and art direction inspired in the work of award-winning directors.
```

### ambiente-quarto · 3:4

```

LENS: 50mm at f/4, headboard sharp, bed linen softening at frame-bottom.
LIGHT: Low evening light at 2900K from two integrated reading lights in the headboard panel, ambient daylight fading from a window camera-right at 6000K. Amber pools on the wood, deep but not crushed shadows.
SUBJECT: Bedroom with a full-width custom headboard wall in dark walnut veneer with a slim recessed niche and integrated warm light, floating nightstands in the same walnut, linen bedding in off-white.

MIDGROUND: Walnut headboard panel with continuous horizontal grain, light grazing its texture.
BACKGROUND: Deep green wall paint at the edges, sheer curtain.
MATERIAL BEHAVIOR: Walnut satin finish, grain glowing under grazing light.
POST BEHAVIOR: Warm color balance, medium contrast, slight lift in the shadows, no visible grain.
COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Headboard as horizontal band across the middle third, light niche off-center right.
MOOD & ART DIRECTION: Composition and art direction inspired in the work of award-winning directors.

### material-ferragem · 4:5

```
CAMERA: Macro setup at ISO 100, tripod, lens axis at 30 degrees to a cabinet door edge.
LENS: 100mm macro at f/8, focus on the hinge and the door edge, background falling soft.
LIGHT: Single soft source at 3200K from camera-right, low and raking, revealing wood grain and metal texture. Shadow side two stops darker.
SUBJECT: Detail of a half-open walnut veneer cabinet door showing a concealed soft-close hinge in matte dark metal and the clean veneered edge banding.
FOREGROUND: Door edge entering from frame-left, sharp.
MIDGROUND: Hinge mechanism, crisp metal edges.
BACKGROUND: Dark interior of the cabinet, warm falloff.
MATERIAL BEHAVIOR: Walnut veneer with tight grain, metal with fine brushed texture.
POST BEHAVIOR: Warm balance, medium-high contrast, deep warm blacks, no grain.
COMPOSITIONAL GEOMETRY: Diagonal door edge from bottom-left to top-right, hinge at the right third.
MOOD & ART DIRECTION: Composition and art direction inspired in the work of award-winning directors.
